When I launched my SEO portfolio, I had no idea what to expect. I had optimised titles, written case studies, and set up Google Search Console (GSC) – but would anyone actually find it?

This post is a transparent breakdown of my portfolio’s first month in GSC. The numbers aren’t huge, but they taught me valuable lessons about how Google discovers new sites.

---

## The Setup

- **Site:** `karandave211.github.io`
- **Launch date:** Early April 2026
- **Content at launch:** 4 case studies + 2 blog posts + homepage + about + contact
- **Technical:** Sitemap submitted, robots.txt default, no backlinks (yet)

I tracked data from **April 1 – April 30, 2026** (first full month after indexing began).

---

## The Numbers (Aggregated)

| Metric | Total |
|--------|-------|
| **Impressions** | 47 |
| **Clicks** | 3 |
| **Average CTR** | 6.4% |
| **Average Position** | 9.2 |

*Impressions = number of times my site appeared in search results. Clicks = actual visits from search.*

For a brand new site with zero backlinks, this is… normal. Most new sites get fewer than 100 impressions in month one.

---

## Top 5 Queries That Drove Impressions

I exported the performance report and filtered by impressions. Here were the top five search terms (anonymised slightly):

| Query | Impressions | Clicks | Position |
|-------|-------------|--------|----------|
| "ahmedabad cafe seo audit" | 12 | 1 | 6.0 |
| "karan dave seo" (branded) | 8 | 1 | 1.0 |
| "how to use google search console" | 6 | 0 | 12.5 |
| "local seo checklist" | 5 | 0 | 14.0 |
| "ev adoption ahmedabad" | 4 | 0 | 8.3 |

**Observations:**
- Branded query (`karan dave seo`) performed best (position 1) – but only 1 click.
- The cafe case study (which I optimised heavily for local keywords) generated the most impressions.
- Informational queries (`how to use google search console`) attracted impressions but no clicks because my content was not yet ranking high enough.

---

## What the Data Taught Me

### 1. Branded searches are valuable but low volume
People who know my name already find me easily. But that audience is tiny. To grow, I need to rank for non‑branded, problem‑based keywords.

### 2. Long‑tail local keywords work (slowly)
`"ahmedabad cafe seo audit"` had 12 impressions and 1 click – a ~8% CTR. That’s decent for a position 6 result. With better internal linking and a stronger meta description, I might push it into top 3.

### 3. Informational content gets impressions but needs better snippets
The Search Console guide got 6 impressions but 0 clicks. Why? The title and meta description were generic. I rewrote them after noticing this and will monitor next month.

### 4. Position matters more than volume
A position 1 branded query brought 8 impressions and 1 click. A position 6 query brought 12 impressions and 1 click. CTR drops sharply as position worsens – which is normal.

---

## Actions I Took Based on This Data

- **Rewrote meta descriptions** for the underperforming pages.
- **Added internal links** from high‑impression pages to related content.
- **Submitted a few URLs for re‑indexing** using GSC’s URL Inspection tool.
- **Created a “Sitemap” reminder** to check that Google was finding all new posts (it was).

---

## What I’d Do Differently Next Time

1. **Start tracking from day one** – I didn't, and I lost the first few days of data.
2. **Add UTM parameters** to external links earlier – to distinguish organic vs referral traffic.
3. **Focus on 3–5 core keywords** rather than spreading too thin.

---

## What You Should Expect for Your Own Site

If you launch a new portfolio or blog:

| Time since launch | Typical impressions (monthly) | Typical clicks |
|-------------------|-------------------------------|----------------|
| Month 1 | 20 – 200 | 0 – 10 |
| Month 2 (with some backlinks) | 100 – 500 | 5 – 30 |
| Month 3 (with consistent content) | 300 – 1000 | 20 – 80 |

Don’t be discouraged by low numbers early on. Search engines take time to trust new sites. Focus on quality content, internal linking, and one or two backlinks.

---

## Final Thoughts

My first month in Search Console wasn’t impressive by any external metric. But it gave me real data to work with – and that data told a clear story: people are searching for local SEO and case studies; I just need to give them better reasons to click.

If you’re starting out, don’t obsess over traffic. Obsess over understanding your data. The clicks will come later.
