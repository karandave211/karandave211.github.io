### 🛵 Consumer Purchase Factors for EVs in Ahmedabad
**Dissertation Project | BBA Final Year**

This research investigates the critical factors influencing why consumers in Ahmedabad choose electric two-wheelers. 

* **Market Scope:** Two-wheeler segment in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
* **Key Drivers:** Battery range, infrastructure availability, and subsidy awareness.
* **Methodology:** Primary research via consumer surveys across major urban hubs.
