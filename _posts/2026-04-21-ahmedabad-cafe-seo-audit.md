## The Client

A mid-sized cafe located in **Satellite, Ahmedabad**. They had been operating for 2 years but were getting almost no organic traffic from Google. Most customers found them through word of mouth or by walking past.

**The Problem:** When people searched "best cafe near me" or "cafe in Satellite Ahmedabad", this cafe did not appear on the first 5 pages of Google.

---

## Step 1: Technical SEO Audit

I ran a full technical audit using free tools: Google Search Console, PageSpeed Insights, and Screaming Frog (free version).

### Issues Found:

| Issue | Severity |
|-------|----------|
| Missing meta descriptions on all pages | High |
| Slow mobile load time (6.2 seconds) | Critical |
| No schema markup for local business | High |
| Broken images on menu page | Medium |
| No XML sitemap submitted to Google | Medium |
| Missing alt text on 12 images | Low |

### Fixes Applied:

- Added unique meta descriptions to all 8 pages
- Compressed images and enabled caching → load time improved from 6.2s to 2.8s
- Added LocalBusiness schema markup
- Fixed broken image links
- Generated and submitted XML sitemap via Jekyll plugin

---

## Step 2: Keyword Research

I identified **35 local keywords** with high intent and low to medium competition.

### Target Keywords Selected:

| Keyword | Search Volume (Ahmedabad) | Difficulty | Current Rank |
|---------|--------------------------|------------|---------------|
| cafe in Satellite Ahmedabad | 500/mo | Low | Not in top 50 |
| best coffee Ahmedabad | 1,200/mo | Medium | Page 4 |
| breakfast near Prahlad Nagar | 800/mo | Low | Not found |
| work-friendly cafe Ahmedabad | 400/mo | Low | Page 6 |
| cold coffee delivery Satellite | 300/mo | Low | Page 5 |

### Primary Keyword Chosen: **"cafe in Satellite Ahmedabad"**

**Why:** High relevance to the cafe's location, decent search volume, and low competition.

---

## Step 3: On-Page SEO Optimization

### Changes Made to the Website:

1. **Title Tags** – Rewrote all 8 title tags to include primary + secondary keywords
   - Before: `"Home | Cafe Name"`
   - After: `"Cafe Name – Best Cafe in Satellite Ahmedabad | Coffee & Breakfast"`

2. **Meta Descriptions** – Added compelling descriptions with CTAs

3. **Header Tags** – Restructured H1, H2, H3 hierarchy
   - H1: Main page title with keyword
   - H2: Section headers
   - H3: Sub-sections

4. **Content Updates** – Added location-specific content
   - Created "Cafe near Prahlad Nagar" section
   - Added "Cafe near ISKCON Temple" section
   - Included nearby landmark references

5. **Image Optimization** – Added alt text to all images, compressed file sizes

## Key Takeaways

1. **Local SEO works fast** – Saw movement within 2 weeks for low-competition keywords
2. **GBP is non-negotiable** – Complete profile + active engagement directly = rankings + calls
3. **Mobile speed matters** – After fixing load time (6s → 2.8s), bounce rate dropped 23%
4. **Location pages work** – Creating pages targeting specific areas (Prahlad Nagar, ISKCON) ranked faster than generic pages
5. **Reviews = trust** – Responding to every review (even old ones) improved perceived business activity

---

## What I'd Do Differently Next Time

- Start link building earlier (takes 4-6 weeks to see impact)
- Track call conversions from GBP (forgot to set up call tracking)
- Set up Google Tag Manager for better event tracking
- Create more visual content (infographics of audit process)
