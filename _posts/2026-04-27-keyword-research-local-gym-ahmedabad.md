## The Challenge

A local gym in Ahmedabad wanted to attract more members through organic search but had no idea which keywords to target. Their current website ranked only for the brand name. I conducted a keyword research exercise to identify opportunities that could bring in ready‑to‑join traffic.

This post walks through my exact process using free tools only.

---

## Step 1: Seed Keywords

I started with five core terms that describe the gym's services:

- gym in Ahmedabad
- fitness center Ahmedabad
- personal trainer Satellite (a specific neighbourhood)
- Zumba classes Ahmedabad
- cheapest gym near me

These came from brainstorming and looking at competitor headings.

---

## Step 2: Expand Using Google Autocomplete & Related Searches

I typed each seed keyword into Google (in incognito mode) and noted the suggested completions and "People also ask" boxes.

Example for *"gym in Ahmedabad"* gave me:
- gym in Ahmedabad with fees
- gym in Ahmedabad for ladies
- 24/7 gym Ahmedabad

I collected 45 raw keyword ideas in 20 minutes.

---

## Step 3: Filter with Free Tools (Google Keyword Planner)

I used **Google Keyword Planner** (free with a Google Ads account – no spend required) to get search volume and competition data.

I entered the 45 keywords and focused on:

- **Search volume** – between 50 and 1000 per month
- **Competition** – low or medium
- **Commercial intent** – terms like "join gym", "fees", "personal trainer"

---

## Step 4: The Final Keyword List (Top 5)

After filtering, I recommended targeting these keywords initially:

| Keyword | Monthly Searches (Ahmedabad) | Competition | Intent |
|---------|------------------------------|-------------|--------|
| gym in Ahmedabad with fees | 320 | Low | High |
| ladies gym Satellite | 180 | Low | High |
| 24/7 gym Ahmedabad | 140 | Medium | Medium |
| personal trainer near Prahlad Nagar | 90 | Low | High |
| cheapest gym membership Ahmedabad | 210 | Medium | High |

---

## Step 5: Actionable Recommendations

For each keyword, I suggested a specific page on the gym's website:

- **Ladies gym Satellite** → create a dedicated "Women's Fitness" page
- **24/7 gym Ahmedabad** → highlight round‑the‑clock access on homepage and a separate page
- **Personal trainer near Prahlad Nagar** → build a "Trainers" page with local area mentions

I also recommended optimising the Google Business Profile to include these keywords in the description and services.

---

## Results So Far (4 weeks)

The gym implemented only two of the suggestions (they started small):
- Created a "Women's Fitness" page optimised for *ladies gym Satellite*
- Updated GBP description

Within 4 weeks:
- The page ranks on page 2 for its target keyword (up from not being indexed)
- GBP direction requests increased by 35%
- One phone inquiry specifically mentioned finding them for "ladies gym"

Full impact will be measured at 90 days.

---

## Tools Used

- Google Keyword Planner (free)
- Google Autocomplete (free)
- Google Search Console (to check current rankings)

---

## Takeaway for Local Businesses

You don't need expensive tools to find valuable keywords. Start with seed terms, listen to Google's suggestions, and filter by commercial intent. Even implementing 2–3 targeted keywords can bring observable traffic and calls.
